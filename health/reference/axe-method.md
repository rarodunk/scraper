# Part I — The Published Frameworks

## 1. Ancient Remedies — the parent philosophy

The organizing idea across all of Axe's work: modern medicine treats symptoms
downstream; the traditional systems — **Traditional Chinese Medicine, Ayurveda,
and Greek/humoral medicine** — identified and treated the *root cause*. His
stated foundations:

- **Eat right for your type.** Constitution-based eating (TCM five elements /
  Ayurvedic dosha) rather than one diet for everyone.
- **Live in sync with your circadian clock.** Meals, light, sleep, and activity
  timed to the day and the season.
- **Root cause over symptom.** Ask what upstream failure produced the symptom.
- **The toolkit:** medicinal herbs, functional mushrooms, essential oils, CBD,
  bone broth and collagen, fermented foods, and restorative mind-body practice.

## 2. The 4R Gut Protocol

Axe's signature gut framework, and the backbone of *Eat Dirt*. Gut integrity is
treated as upstream of nearly everything — including nutrient status, which is
exactly why it matters when reading a lab panel with several nutrients low.

| Step | What it means | Typical tools |
|---|---|---|
| **1. REMOVE** | Take out what damages the gut lining | Refined sugar, industrial seed oils, alcohol, ultra-processed food, and personal triggers (commonly gluten, conventional dairy, soy); NSAIDs where a doctor agrees; treat infections — H. pylori, SIBO, parasites, candida |
| **2. REPLACE** | Put in what the gut needs to work | Bone broth, cooked vegetables, healthy fats, clean protein; **digestive enzymes and bitters**; betaine HCl where indicated; adequate stomach acid and bile |
| **3. REPAIR** | Rebuild the intestinal lining | **L-glutamine**, collagen and bone broth, zinc carnosine, DGL licorice, aloe vera, slippery elm, marshmallow root, **butyric acid**, omega-3s, vitamin D |
| **4. REBALANCE** | Reseed the microbiome | **Soil-based organism (SBO) probiotics** — the "eat dirt" thesis; fermented foods (sauerkraut, kimchi, kefir, yogurt); prebiotic fiber and resistant starch |

## 3. The Cell Membrane Model

From his cellular-health work with Dr. Will Cole and guests: **the cell membrane
is the gatekeeper.** It controls what nutrients enter and what toxins leave, and
it carries the receptors that hormones dock onto. A stiff, damaged, or
oxidized membrane means hormones signal poorly and nutrients enter poorly —
*regardless of how good the bloodstream supply is*.

- Membranes are built from **phospholipids** (phosphatidylcholine) and
  **omega-3 fatty acids** (EPA/DHA).
- **Industrial seed oils** (soybean, corn, canola, cottonseed, safflower,
  sunflower, grapeseed) are the named antagonist — high in oxidation-prone
  linoleic acid, which gets built into membranes and peroxidizes.
- **Superoxide dismutase (SOD)** sits on the membrane as a frontline antioxidant.
- Practical: eat egg yolks, liver, wild fatty fish, olive oil, avocado, ghee;
  supplement phosphatidylcholine/krill or fish oil; eliminate seed oils.

## 4. The Cellular Detox Stack

Axe's cell-detox approach, as published:
- **L-glutathione** — the master intracellular antioxidant (liposomal, or NAC as
  precursor)
- **Superoxide dismutase (SOD)**
- **Chlorella** — binds mercury, lead, cadmium, and uranium in the gut and
  prevents reabsorption
- Plus: milk thistle, cilantro, cruciferous vegetables and sulforaphane,
  activated charcoal or modified citrus pectin as binders, infrared sauna

> **Sequencing rule that Axe and every competent detox practitioner share:
> open drainage BEFORE you mobilize anything.** Mobilizing toxins with
> constipated bowels, sluggish bile, and poor sweating just recirculates them and
> makes people feel awful. Bowels moving daily → bile flowing → lymph moving →
> sweating → *then* bind and mobilize. Never reverse this order.

# Part II — The 4-Month Cellular Protocol (Reconstruction)

Read the sourcing note at the top of this file before using this.

The arc is **Reset → Repair → Detoxify → Regenerate**. The logic is strictly
sequential and the order is the point: you cannot repair a gut while the
irritants are still arriving, and you must not mobilize toxins into a body that
cannot drain or rebuild.

---

## MONTH 1 — CELLULAR RESET
*Goal: stop the damage, open the drains, stabilize the terrain.*

Nothing here is exotic. Month 1 is about **subtraction and stabilization**, and
it is the month people most want to skip and most need.

**A. Remove the cellular load**
- **All industrial seed oils** — soybean, corn, canola, cottonseed, safflower,
  sunflower, grapeseed. Cook in olive oil, ghee, butter, coconut, or tallow.
  This is the single highest-leverage removal in the whole protocol.
- Refined sugar and refined flour; ultra-processed food and artificial additives
- Alcohol — out entirely for the month
- Common personal triggers, trialed out: gluten, conventional dairy, soy
- Plastic food and drink storage; conventional cleaning and personal-care
  products; filter drinking water

**B. Open drainage — before any detox, in this order**
1. **Bowels moving daily.** Non-negotiable prerequisite. Magnesium citrate
   150–400 mg at night, 30–40 g fiber, 2.5–3 L water, Triphala 1/2–1 tsp at bed.
2. **Bile flowing.** Bitter greens daily (dandelion, arugula, radicchio),
   beets, artichoke, lemon water on waking, castor oil packs 3–4x/week.
3. **Lymph moving.** Dry brushing before showers, rebounding or walking 20–30
   min daily, deep diaphragmatic breathing.
4. **Sweating.** Sauna or hot bath with Epsom salts 3–4x/week.
5. **Kidneys.** Adequate water plus **electrolytes** — plain water without
   minerals is not hydration.

**C. Stabilize blood sugar and circadian rhythm**
- **Protein-forward breakfast within 60–90 minutes of waking**, 30–40 g
- **Morning sunlight, 10–20 min within an hour of waking, no sunglasses**
- Stop eating 3 hours before bed; consistent sleep and wake times
- Largest meal at midday when digestive fire peaks (Ayurvedic, and it matches
  modern chrononutrition)

**D. Begin 4R steps 1 and 2** — Remove and Replace. Bone broth daily (1–2 cups),
cooked vegetables over raw, digestive bitters before meals.

**E. Start membrane repair** — omega-3s, egg yolks, olive oil, avocado.

## MONTH 2 — REPAIR & REBUILD
*Goal: seal the gut lining, rebuild membranes, replete what the labs showed low.*

Now that irritants are out and drainage is open, repair holds instead of leaking
away. **This is the month deficiencies actually get corrected** — because now
they will absorb.

**A. 4R steps 3 and 4 — Repair and Rebalance**
- **L-glutamine** 5 g daily, away from food
- **Collagen peptides** 10–20 g daily, or continued bone broth
- **Zinc carnosine** 75 mg daily
- **DGL licorice** before meals; aloe vera, slippery elm, marshmallow root
- **SBO probiotic** — start low, build up; add fermented foods daily
- **Butyric acid**; prebiotic fiber and resistant starch

**B. Targeted nutrient repletion — driven by the labs, not by a template.**
This is where the specific deficiencies found on the panel get corrected, using
the correct chemical forms, correct timing, and correct absorption pairings.
See `marker-playbook.md` for per-marker dosing.

**C. Membrane rebuild** — phosphatidylcholine or krill oil, EPA/DHA 2–3 g daily,
continued zero seed oils.

**D. Digestive capacity** — bitters, enzymes, betaine HCl if indicated.

---

## MONTH 3 — DETOXIFY & MOBILIZE
*Goal: now that drainage is open and the gut is sealed, clear the accumulated load.*

**Prerequisite check — do not begin until all are true:** daily bowel movements,
good energy, gut symptoms settled, sweating regularly, and nutrient repletion
underway. **If any are false, extend Month 2. The calendar does not outrank the
body.**

- **Glutathione** (liposomal 500 mg) or **NAC** 600–1200 mg
- **Sulforaphane** — broccoli sprouts, or supplement; the strongest known
  natural inducer of the body's own Phase II detox enzymes
- **Milk thistle** 150–300 mg silymarin; **cruciferous vegetables** daily
- **Binders, away from food and all supplements by 2+ hours** — chlorella
  (start 500 mg, build), activated charcoal, modified citrus pectin
- **Methylation support** — methylfolate, methyl-B12, B6 as P5P, betaine/TMG
- **Infrared sauna** 3–4x/week, castor oil packs, dry brushing
- **Hydration and electrolytes throughout** — mobilization without dilution and
  elimination is the classic way to feel terrible

> **Go slow.** Detox reactions (headache, fatigue, irritability, rash) mean you
> mobilized faster than you can clear. Back off, increase binders and water, and
> confirm the bowels are moving. Aggressive detox is not a virtue.

## MONTH 4 — REGENERATE & OPTIMIZE
*Goal: build new mitochondria, trigger autophagy, retest, and make it permanent.*

- **Mitochondrial support** — CoQ10 as ubiquinol 100–200 mg, PQQ 20 mg,
  magnesium, B-complex, alpha-lipoic acid, creatine 3–5 g daily
- **Autophagy triggers** — time-restricted eating in a 12–14 hour window
  (**not** a long fast in anyone under-fueled, iron-deficient, or with low
  cortisol — for them this step is deferred, not attempted)
- **Hormesis** — zone 2 cardio 150+ min/week, resistance training 2–3x/week,
  sauna, cold exposure
- **Adaptogens matched to constitution** — ashwagandha, holy basil, reishi,
  cordyceps
- **RETEST the panel** and compare against baseline, marker by marker
- **Transition to maintenance** — "eat right for your type," circadian living,
  seasonal eating
- **Purpose, prayer/meditation, community** — Axe treats these as biological
  inputs with measurable effects, and they belong in the protocol, not as a
  footnote

# Part III — How to Apply This to a Real Person

**The protocol is a template. The panel is the person. When they conflict, the
person wins.** Adapt on these rules:

1. **Never run a restrictive Month 1 on someone who is under-fueled.** If the
   panel shows low total cholesterol, low morning cortisol, low ferritin, and a
   low-normal A1c, that person needs *addition*, not subtraction. Keep the
   removals (seed oils, sugar, alcohol — those cost nothing) and **drop the
   caloric restriction and any fasting entirely.** Reframe Month 1 as
   **"Reset and Refuel."**

2. **Never run an aggressive Month 3 detox on someone with low ferritin, low
   cortisol, or an unexplained kidney marker.** Detoxification is metabolically
   expensive and is cleared through the liver and kidneys. A depleted body should
   be *built* before it is *cleared*. Move gentle drainage support into Months 1–2
   and defer active mobilization until ferritin and cortisol have recovered —
   often Month 4 or later. Say this explicitly rather than quietly reordering.

3. **Any RED marker goes to a physician in week 1, in parallel with everything
   else.** The protocol never substitutes for a workup.

4. **Sequence nutrient repletion by dependency.** Some corrections gate others:
   iron gates thyroid (TPO is a heme enzyme); magnesium gates vitamin D
   activation; stomach acid gates both iron and B12; cholesterol gates cortisol
   and vitamin D. Fix the gate first or the downstream work is wasted.

5. **Retest before escalating.** Eight to twelve weeks for most markers. If a
   number has not moved, the answer is almost never "take more" — it is
   "absorption is still blocked" or "the root cause is still unaddressed."
