## The 5 things that matter most

### 1. Iron — this is the big one

Your ferritin (stored iron) is **21**. It should be 70–90. Below 30 is deficient.

This one number likely explains fatigue that sleep doesn't fix, hair shedding,
cold hands and feet, brain fog, and low motivation. It's also dragging your
thyroid down, because the enzyme that builds thyroid hormone needs iron to work.

> **Ferrous bisglycinate, 25–30 mg elemental iron.**
> **Every OTHER morning, 7:00am, empty stomach, with 500 mg vitamin C.**

**Every other day — not daily.** A dose of iron shuts down your absorption for
about 24 hours. Taking it daily means every second dose is wasted. Alternating
absorbs roughly **twice** as much.

- Look for **"iron bisglycinate chelate"** or **"gentle iron"** — not ferrous
  sulfate, which is what makes people quit
- Gentler alternative: **Floradix** or **Floravital** liquid, 10 ml
- Take with 500 mg plain vitamin C, or 1 tsp **amla powder** in water
- **Nothing else for 60 minutes.** Then breakfast.

⚠️ Get the full iron panel before committing long-term. Retest at 12 weeks. Stop
if ferritin goes above 150. **Store it locked away from children** — iron is the
leading cause of fatal poisoning in young kids.

**This takes 6–12 months.** Don't judge it at week four.

### 2. Move your coffee — free, and worth as much as the supplement

**No coffee or tea within 2 hours either side of iron or an iron-rich meal.**
The tannins block up to 60–90% of the iron in that meal.

Not less coffee. Just later. **Iron at 7am → coffee from 9am is fine.**

**And 10–15 minutes before lunch and dinner:** digestive bitters (gentian,
dandelion, artichoke), 1–2 ml on the tongue — **they have to be tasted**, capsules
don't work — or **1 tbsp apple cider vinegar** in water.

This opens the gate that's blocking your iron, your B12, *and* your fat.

### 3. Eat more fat — your cholesterol is too low

Total cholesterol of **111** sounds like good news. It isn't. Cholesterol is the
raw material your body uses to build **cortisol, oestrogen, progesterone, and
vitamin D**. At 111, you're asking a bakery for bread with no flour.

> **Breakfast by 8:30am, within 90 minutes of waking. 30–40 g protein:**
> **2–3 pastured egg yolks** cooked in butter or ghee · **½–1 avocado** ·
> **1–2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil**

Then olive oil on everything, butter or ghee for cooking, and **1–2 cups bone
broth daily**.

**No fasting. No skipped meals. No calorie counting.** For the next four months
you're adding, not subtracting.

### 4. Vitamin D — never on its own

Yours is **42.85**; optimal is 50–80.

> **With your largest meal of the day:**
> **D3 5,000 IU** (oil-based softgel) · **K2 as MK-7, 100–200 µg** ·
> **Magnesium glycinate 200–400 mg** (move this one to bedtime)

**Why all three:** D pulls calcium into your blood. **K2 directs that calcium
into your bones instead of your arteries.** Magnesium is what switches D into its
active form — a vitamin D level that won't budge is very often a magnesium
problem.

Plus **15–30 minutes of midday sun** on bare arms and legs, stopping before any
pinkness. Retest at 12 weeks; don't exceed 10,000 IU/day without monitoring.

### 5. B vitamins and beets

Your homocysteine is **8.13**; it should be under 7. That means you're short on
B12, folate, B6 or B2.

> **With breakfast: a methylated B-complex** containing methylfolate (5-MTHF)
> 400–800 µg, methylcobalamin 1,000 µg, P5P (B6) 25–50 mg, riboflavin 25–50 mg.

**Methylated forms specifically** — not folic acid, not cyanocobalamin. A common
gene variant converts the synthetic versions poorly.

**Plus ½ cup beets or beet juice daily.** Beets contain betaine, which clears
homocysteine directly. Best single food for this marker.

### Six absorption rules — these are free and they work

1. **Vitamin C with every plant-iron meal.** Bell pepper, citrus, amla, or a
   500 mg tablet.
2. **No coffee or tea within 2 hours of iron.**
3. **A little meat alongside beans or lentils** raises the iron you absorb from
   the plants too.
4. **Soak or sprout grains and beans.** Unsoaked, they carry iron and zinc out of
   you. Sourdough bread is fine; quick-rise bread isn't.
5. **Cook tomato sauce, chili and stews in bare cast iron.** Measurable, and free.
6. **Eat sitting down, unhurried, off a screen.** You genuinely can't digest in a
   rush — stomach acid only flows when you're relaxed.

## The chain: eleven numbers, one story

These aren't separate problems. They're a sequence.

1. **Low iron → slow thyroid.** The enzyme that builds thyroid hormone runs on
   iron. Less iron, less hormone — so your TSH climbed to **2.78** as your
   pituitary started shouting louder.
2. **Slow thyroid → the kidney marker.** An underactive thyroid raises cystatin C
   on its own. That's probably the **1.17**.
3. **Low cholesterol → low cortisol.** Cortisol is built from cholesterol. Thin
   raw material, and morning cortisol lands at **6.6**.
4. **Low cholesterol → low vitamin D.** Your skin makes vitamin D out of a
   cholesterol molecule. Less to work with, less vitamin D — **42.85**.
5. **Poor absorption → high homocysteine.** B12 needs stomach acid to be absorbed.
   The same closed gate blocking your iron is likely blocking your B12 — hence
   **8.13**.

**The leverage is iron and fat.** Fix those two properly and there's a real chance
the thyroid, the kidney marker, the cortisol and the vitamin D all move without
being treated directly.

## What the old traditions called this

Every literate medical tradition described this exact picture long before anyone
could measure ferritin.

**Chinese medicine** calls it **Blood Deficiency with Spleen Qi Deficiency** —
low iron *plus* weak digestion. Their view: the Spleen makes Blood *from* food, so
treating the blood without repairing the digestion is pouring into a leaking
vessel. The classical formula for exactly this pair is ***Ba Zhen Tang***, "Eight
Treasure Decoction," which does both at once.

**Ayurveda** calls iron deficiency ***pandu roga***, "the pallor disease,"
described about two thousand years ago. Its treatment: iron, given **together
with amla** — the richest whole-food vitamin C — plus herbs to rebuild digestive
fire. That's iron plus vitamin C plus gut repair, arrived at two millennia early.
It's also why amla is on your shopping list.

Ayurveda would read you as depleted and cold, and would **explicitly forbid
fasting or cleansing in this state.** Warm, oily, nourishing food instead. That's
exactly the change made to your four-month plan.

**Western folk herbalism** used **nettle, yellow dock, dandelion, blackstrap
molasses and cast-iron cookware.** Yellow dock is the clever one — barely any iron
itself, but as a bitter it switches on stomach acid, improving the iron you absorb
from everything else on the plate.

### Optional additions, with a practitioner

- **Nettle leaf long infusion** — 1 oz dried leaf in 1 quart just-boiled water,
  covered, **steeped 4 hours to overnight**. The long steep is the whole point;
  a 5-minute teabag gets almost nothing. 1–2 cups daily.
- **Ashwagandha 300–600 mg** in the evening, for stress and thyroid support.
  ⚠️ May raise thyroid hormone — helpful here, but retest rather than assume.
  Avoid in pregnancy; caution with autoimmune conditions.
- **Shatavari, amla, triphala** — the Ayurvedic trio for a depleted female system.
- ⚠️ **Licorice root: only under supervision.** It genuinely raises low cortisol,
  but the same mechanism causes **high blood pressure and dangerously low
  potassium.** Not one to self-prescribe.
- ⚠️ **Avoid He Shou Wu** — common in blood-building formulas, documented cases of
  severe liver injury.
- ⚠️ **Avoid high-dose kelp.** Too much iodine can trigger thyroid disease,
  and you don't yet know your antibody status. Food-level seaweed only.

### Change 1 — no restriction in Month 1

Low cholesterol, low cortisol, low ferritin and low-normal blood sugar all say
your body already thinks food is scarce. **Restricting further deepens every one
of those numbers.**

Keep the removals that cost you nothing — **seed oils** (soybean, corn, canola,

ultra-processed food. Cook in olive oil, butter, ghee, coconut or tallow instead.

**But drop the fasting and the calorie counting entirely.**

### Change 2 — the Month 3 detox is postponed

Four reasons, and the fourth is the one people miss:

1. **The cystatin C is unexplained.** Don't push a kidney of uncertain function
   through a detox.
2. **Low cortisol.** Detox is a stressor, and yours is already running thin.
3. **Low ferritin.** Detox is expensive and you have no reserve to spend.
4. **Binders bind iron.** Chlorella, charcoal and citrus pectin can't tell mercury
   from the iron you're trying to absorb. **Running binders during iron repletion
   sabotages the single most important fix on your panel.**

Gentle drainage — daily bowel movements, bitters, sauna, walking — runs from week
one anyway. That's the part that actually matters.

### If you train hard

Low iron plus low cortisol means your body can't absorb hard training right now.
**Swap intense cardio for strength work 2–3× a week plus walking** for these four
months. Push through this state and ferritin goes *down*, not up. It's temporary.
